# Look-alike audit, part 1 (names and words). Read by scripts/lookalike-check.py.
#
# Marks and product or feature names that belong to someone else. Naming them as the name of something
# ParchaOS ships is a trademark and confusion risk; stating a fact about them (credits, legal notices,
# "an Apple-silicon Mac") is fine and goes in scripts/lookalike-allow.txt with a reason.
#
# Format:   level  <tab-or-spaces>  regular expression (case-insensitive, matched on whole lines)
#   deny    a hit that is not in the allow-list fails the check
#   review  a hit is listed (and counted) but does not fail; the owner reviews these at each audit
#
# Keep this list reviewed by the owner. Add a term when a new product or feature name turns up.

# --- Apple marks and product names

deny    \bapple\b

deny    \b(sequoia|sonoma|ventura|monterey|big sur|catalina|mojave|tahoe)\b
deny    \b(sf pro|sf symbols|san francisco font|retina)\b
deny    \b(sidecar|universal control|continuity camera)\b
# --- Other well-known marks that sit close to what ParchaOS ships
