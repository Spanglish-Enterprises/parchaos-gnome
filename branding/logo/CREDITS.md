# ParchaOS logo

The ParchaOS mark -- a halved passion fruit seen from above, with a rind
ring, a pith ring, the pulp and seven seeds -- is original artwork made
for ParchaOS, (c) 2026 Spanglish Enterprises LLC, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
(`LICENSE-ARTWORK`). The name and mark identify ParchaOS; the license
covers the artwork, not permission to present something else as
ParchaOS.

Every logo file in this repository is generated from one design by
`make-brand.py` (run from the repository root). Change the design there
and regenerate, rather than editing the images:

- `parchaos-mark.svg` (full color) and `parchaos-mark-mono.svg` (one
  color)
- `parcha-logo-{black,white}.png`, `parcha-silhouette-{black,white}.png`
  (one-color rasters; the file names are older than the current design)
- the logo and symbolic icons, the menu-bar glyph and the shell theme's
  Activities button, the login-screen and boot-splash word marks, the
  installer branding and the wallpaper (paths in `make-brand.py`)

History: until 2026-09-26 ParchaOS used an adaptation of a third-party
icon (CC BY 3.0). It was replaced everywhere by this original mark.
